Pulseguard Privacy Policy

Last updated August 5, 2026.

Pulseguard uses Google OAuth to authenticate users and, with explicit consent, read Gmail message metadata needed to detect expected email. Pulseguard searches sender, subject, and arrival time. It does not request, read, or store message bodies or attachments.

Data stored

Pulseguard stores your Google email address, monitor rules, alert recipients, message identifiers, receipt timestamps, and an encrypted Google refresh token. Provider credentials and encryption keys are stored as runtime secrets.

How data is used

Google data is used only to determine whether an email matching a monitor arrived before its deadline. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared except with infrastructure providers required to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Monitor history is retained for operating and diagnosing alerts. You may request deletion of your monitors, connection, and stored history by contacting the operator.

Security

OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-GCM before database storage. Access is restricted to allowlisted Google accounts.
